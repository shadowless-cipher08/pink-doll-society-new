ORDERS & PRODUCTS
How do I place an order?
Browse our collection, select your preferred product and available options, and follow the checkout instructions. Your order is not confirmed until the checkout process provides confirmation.
What hair lengths and textures will be available?
Available lengths, textures, and product options should be listed on each product page. Please review the specific listing before ordering, as options may vary by product.
How do I choose the right hair length?
Hair length can look different depending on texture, curl pattern, and how the hair is measured. Check the product's length guide and texture details before making your selection.
Will the products look exactly like the photos?
Product images are intended to show the style and appearance of each item. Lighting, screen settings, styling, and texture can affect how hair appears in photographs.

HAIR CARE
How should I care for my hair?
Follow the care instructions provided for your specific product. In general, use gentle detangling techniques, avoid unnecessary pulling, and follow the manufacturer's guidance for washing, heat styling, and storage.
Can I use heat styling tools?
Heat tolerance depends on the product's material and specifications. Check the manufacturer's instructions before applying heat. Do not assume every texture or product can safely tolerate the same temperature.
